# D&D Life Manager

**Live app:** https://corrida-repos.github.io/dnd-side-app/

A tracker for every nitty-gritty part of D&D 5.5e life that isn't tied to one campaign or adventure and doesn't run on combat's seconds-long timescale: time, travel, weather, survival, rests, upkeep, money, downtime and the wider world. It follows the 5.5e core and supplemental books from Wizards of the Coast, labels legacy and table rules, and describes what the characters notice when their condition changes. It runs in the browser, needs no server, and saves to local storage.

## Running a session

The app is arranged the way a session runs:

- **At the table:** the Dashboard (what needs attention, the party with their HP, conditions, weather, rests and the latest events), Travel, Camp & Survival, Encounters, and Effects & Ailments. The bar at the top of every page holds the clock, time steps, More time, rests, Undo and the attention count.
- **Party:** Player checklists and Characters. Characters holds each character's whole sheet: pick one person, or one section (HP & gear, Status & sheet, Spells & tasks) to compare the party.
- **Between adventures:** Downtime, Bastions, Town & Money, and Storage.
- **World:** the World (the calendar and date, moons, factions, rivals, patron, regions), the Chronicle, Ways Around, and Settings.

A control bar stays on screen on every tab: the time, +10 minutes, +1 hour, +4 hours, **More time** (any number of minutes, skip to 06:00, noon or 18:00, or set the date), Short and Long Rests, **Undo** (also Ctrl/Cmd+Z, outside text fields) and a count of things to handle. The Dashboard's **Needs attention** list gathers saves to roll, dying characters, hunger and thirst late in the day, guttering lights, Bastion turns and decisions, finished downtime awaiting its outcome, unresolved obstacles, overdue debts and restless rivals, each with a button to where it's handled. Secrets such as a hidden disease incubating are marked *DM only* and can be hidden while players can see the screen. Keys 1–4 switch sections. Long tabs open with a **Jump to** row naming their cards, and cards for switched-off optional rules that hold nothing stay folded into one line.

**Player checklists** (Party section, and a summary on the Dashboard) list, per player, the conditions they're suffering with what fixes each one (Exhaustion by cause, poison, disease, curses, jail, low HP, overloaded packs, the penalty after being raised) and upkeep that has real consequences if ignored (food and water today, rations and waterskin running low, unsafe water, no warm cover for a cold night, spells left unprepared, an attuned item out of reach, ammunition to recover or running out, fading charms, debts, finished downtime). Inventory that needs dealing with is flagged too: overfilled containers, an extradimensional container packed inside another (which destroys both), prepared spells whose costly component isn't carried, a spent Reliquary talisman, and (DM only) an attuned item that is secretly cursed. Chores with no game effect are left out. A player view hides DM-only secrets, and each list can be copied to send to that player.

## Downtime and campaign deadlines

Schedule a deadline in **World → Campaign deadlines** with a public warning, an optional private note, and an optional rival. Linked rivals advance on that schedule during downtime and travel. For example, the last ferry might leave while the party is still crafting. **Downtime → Projects → Plan a downtime skip** previews the target date, estimated upkeep, which selected projects could finish, and Bastion turns due. **More time** can also advance directly to the next decision.

A deadline marked for a DM decision pauses clock skips, ordinary travel and downtime. Open it from **Needs attention** to play the scene, accept an offscreen outcome, or defer the decision. Playing the scene switches town play to Day to day. Resume the saved skip when the party returns to downtime. Individual actions that resolve in one step, such as cooking, can finish when a deadline falls during them. The Chronicle records when the deadline came due and how the DM ruled.

## Enemy force

Set up the campaign antagonist under **World → Enemy force**. Choose a custom enemy or an editable preset inspired by the 2024 DMG’s campaign conflicts. Enter the days per stage and what happens if the endgame succeeds. Danger rises from 0 to 5 on the campaign clock, including during downtime and travel. Warning signs appear in skip previews and the Chronicle. The DM can inspect what each operation changes in the world.

A setback moves future operations without erasing what the enemy has already achieved. At the endgame, a skip pauses so the DM can play the scene or accept the offscreen outcome. Marking the enemy force stopped clears its pending operations. Each campaign tracks one main enemy force alongside its independent campaign deadlines.

## Levels of detail

**Settings → Level of detail** offers five cumulative presets. Light includes supported 5.5e player options. Complete adds 5.5e requirements and consequences. The three higher levels add increasingly involved legacy and table rules.

| Tier | What it adds |
| --- | --- |
| **1. Light** | Core tracking for the clock, rests, weather and timers, plus Circle Magic and Bastion facilities from the 2024 DMG, *Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn*, and *Eberron: Forge of the Artificer*. |
| **2. Complete** | Food and water, extreme weather saves, forced marches, navigation checks, encounters, carrying capacity, coin weight, tent capacity, expenses, spell components and consumable gear. |
| **3. Gritty** (new games start here) | Campfire protection, unsafe food and water, weapon and armor training, legacy sidekicks and downtime complications. |
| **4. Hardcore** | Weather modifiers to damage rolls, stricter appraisals and penalties for sleeping in armor. |
| **5. Brutal** | Lingering injuries, madness, and wear on gear and ships. |

You can undo a preset change or adjust individual switches. Encounter book selections, numeric thresholds and DM choices such as allowing extra crafting helpers are campaign settings. Bastions, crafting, diseases, poisons and hunting are available when the table uses them.

## What it tracks



| Area | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **D&D Beyond import** | Paste (or upload) a public character's data from D&D Beyond's character service. It brings in level, class, subclass, species, feats, Eldritch Invocations, size, stats, HP, Hit Point Dice, Con save, Survival, passive Perception, darkvision and resistances; the inventory with real weights, containers, coins and ammunition; magic items with attunement and charges; spells with ritual, casting time and duration; spell and pact slots and limited-use features; and optionally Exhaustion and death saves. Importing again updates the character and keeps everything the tracker owns (food, water, item knowledge, status history, items added by hand). |
| **Time and calendar** | A clock and date (the Eberron Calendar of Galifar by default, with its holidays and the moon of each month, or Harptos, Gregorian or a plain 12×30 calendar), sunrise and sunset by season, and quick buttons to skip ahead. Every hour and every midnight, the rules checks below run. |
| **Weather** | The DMG d20 tables for temperature (normal, or 1d4×10 °F colder or warmer), wind and precipitation, rolled each day from the climate, season and altitude. An hourly temperature curve, rain vs. sleet vs. snow, the rolls shown, and a manual override. |
| **Weather and damage** | Table rule (Hardcore tier, off by default): outdoors, heavy rain favors Lightning and hinders Fire, a thunderstorm favors Thunder, snow and extreme cold favor Cold, extreme heat favors Fire, clear daylight favors Radiant, night favors Necrotic, and swamp air favors Poison. The Underdark favors Necrotic. Favored damage deals +1 per damage die, hindered damage −1 per die (minimum 1). |
| **Weather effects** | Strong wind, heavy rain or snow, extreme cold (DC 10 Con save each hour), extreme heat (DC 5 + 1 per hour without water, Disadvantage in heavy armor), high altitude, and light levels. Saves roll automatically, or wait for players to roll. |
| **Food and water** | Daily needs by size, rations and waterskins, camp stores, foraging (DC 10/15/20), refilling at water sources. Dehydration and malnutrition are checked at midnight, including the rule that Exhaustion can't be removed until the creature has eaten or drunk a full day's worth. |
| **Labelled debuffs** | Every Exhaustion level records its cause (Malnutrition, Dehydration, Extreme cold, Extreme heat, Forced march, Cold night or Other), and raw food or unpurified water shows as **Sick** (Poisoned until a Long Rest, with what caused it). Levels from Malnutrition or Dehydration are marked 🔒 and stay until the need is met in full. A Long Rest removes the newest level that isn't locked. |
| **Temperature control** | Day-to-night temperature swings, a **Bedroll** (automatic success on extreme cold saves while sleeping in it) and **Blanket** (Advantage), and an optional cold-night rule: sleeping out at or below 32 °F (adjustable) without warm cover means a DC 10 Con save on waking or 1 Exhaustion level. Also Cold Resistance or Immunity, cold weather gear, Fire Resistance or Immunity, heavy clothing, shelter (tent, building, cave) and an optional "campfire warms the camp" table rule. |
| **Campfire** | Lighting it is a roll (table rule, switchable): a Wisdom (Survival) check against DC 10, +5 when rain or snow has dampened the fuel, +5 in strong wind or heavy rain (only possible in a sheltered pit) and +5 without a Tinderbox (10 minutes a try instead of 1). *Prestidigitation*, *Druidcraft* or *Fire Bolt* light it with no roll. Fuel burns in hours, and wind and heavy rain put out an unsheltered fire. While it burns it **warms** the resting camp against extreme cold and cold nights down to −20 °F by default (10 °F lower in a sheltered pit, adjustable), **lights** the night watch so watchers aren't penalized for darkness (for as long as the fuel lasts the watch, though a fire at night also draws encounters), and **cooks**: cook all raw food into safe stores (1 hour), boil water safe (30 minutes) or make a hot meal with a Cook's Utensils check. Gathering firewood and Chef feat treats too. |
| **Armor** | Light, Medium or Heavy armor and shields per character, worn or not. Putting it on and taking it off moves the clock (Light 1/1 min, Medium 5/1, Heavy 10/5), and when the whole party does it the clock moves by the slowest person. Medium or Heavy armor gives Disadvantage on extreme heat saves. There's an optional Xanathar's rule for sleeping in armor. |
| **Raw food and dirty water** | Foraged food arrives **raw**. Eating it uncooked means a Con save (DC 10 by default, adjustable), or the character is **Sick**: Poisoned until they finish a Long Rest. Water sources are marked clean or questionable, and questionable or foraged water means a DC 10 Con save or Sick in the same way. Lesser Restoration, Lay on Hands or other magic that ends Poisoned cures it sooner, but **Antitoxin doesn't**: drunk beforehand, it gives Advantage on these saves (and on poison saves) for an hour. Cooking a hot meal uses up raw food first and makes it safe, boiling makes water safe, and *Purify Food and Drink* (as a ritual in 10 minutes or with a slot, with preparation checks and Wizard ritual casting) makes everything safe. Meals use safe food and rations before anything raw. Both risks can be switched off in Settings. |
| **Rests and fatigue** | Short Rest (1 h), Long Rest (8 h, once per 24 h, removes 1 Exhaustion level) with a countdown until the next one is allowed. **Forced march:** each travel hour past 8 since the last Long Rest means a Con save (DC 10 + hours past 8), and the Travel card previews the DCs. **Staying awake** (optional Xanathar's rule, on by default): every 24 hours without a Long Rest means a Con save (DC 10, +5 per further day) or Sleep deprivation Exhaustion, with an "awake for" timer on the Time card. |
| **Morning and spells** | A daily checklist per character, and spell preparation using the 2024 prepared-spell tables. Clerics, Druids and Wizards can change any spell after a Long Rest. Paladins and Rangers swap one. Bards, Sorcerers and Warlocks change spells on level up. Always-prepared spells, ritual and concentration tags. |
| **Ritual casting** | Spells cast as rituals take their casting time + 10 minutes and use no slot. You need the spell prepared, except that Wizards can cast from their spellbook. Ritual tags, casting times and durations come from an imported 5etools spell file, or from a built-in list of common rituals (2024 names like *Tiny Hut* included). Casting normally uses a tracked slot of the right level. Either way the clock moves and the spell's timer starts on the Effects & Ailments tab, with Concentration handled. |
| **Downtime** | Crafting nonmagical items (one 8-hour day per 10 GP of price, materials cost half), with a picker of common armor, weapons and gear that shows its price, craft time and tool, Spell Scrolls, Potions of Healing, magic items by rarity (consumables take half), and custom activities. Several crafters split the time. |
| **Bastions** | Found a Bastion at level 5 and add facilities. Special facilities come with level (2/4/5/6 slots at levels 5/9/13/17) and take orders: Craft, Empower, Harvest, Maintain, Recruit, Research or Trade. Basic facilities cost money and take time to build: Cramped 500 GP and 20 days, Roomy 1,000 GP and 45, Vast 3,000 GP and 125. Enlarging costs 500 GP and 25 days (Cramped to Roomy) or 2,000 GP and 80 days (Roomy to Vast). Construction moves forward each in-game day, a Bastion turn comes due every 7 days, and a d100 Bastion event is rolled when every facility is told to Maintain. |
| **Running a Bastion from afar** | Set how far the owner is from their Bastion. While away, orders are drafted and then sent by messenger hireling (24 miles a day, 2 CP a mile, busy until back), mounted courier (27 miles a day, table rule), *Animal Messenger* (50 miles, ritual), *Sending* (instant, level 3 slot), *Dream* (when the recipient next sleeps, level 5 slot) or a House Sivis message station (Eberron, table rule). Orders take effect only when they arrive; if none reached the Bastion before a turn, its hirelings Maintain (DMG). Each turn's report travels back the same way, and the log records when every message was sent and arrived. |
| **Gear and resources** | HP and Temporary HP, Hit Point Dice (spend on a Short Rest; all back on a Long Rest if you start with at least 1 HP). Limited-use features and spell slots that come back on a Short Rest, a Long Rest, one per Short Rest, or at dawn, with class presets and automatic slot tables for full casters, half casters and Warlocks. Magic items: attunement (a Short Rest focused on the item, maximum 3, ends after 24 hours more than 100 ft away), identifying (by a rest or the *Identify* spell, including the ritual) and charges regained at dawn or on rests. |
| **Dying and unconscious** | At 0 HP a character falls Unconscious and is dying, or dies outright if the leftover damage is at least their HP maximum. Death saves: 10 or higher succeeds, a 1 counts as two failures, and a 20 brings them back with 1 HP. Three successes or three failures decide it, and damage at 0 HP adds a failure. Stabilize with a DC 10 Wisdom (Medicine) check, a Healer's Kit or *Spare the Dying*. A stable character regains 1 HP after 1d4 hours on the clock, or wakes right away when healed. |
| **Identifying magic items** | Unknown items show the party's own description ("silver ring with a blue stone") until identified. Each item lists hidden facts (properties, attunement, charges, curses) and a log of how each was learned. *Detect Magic* reveals that it's magical and its school, Arcana checks (table rule: DC 10/15/20/25/30 by rarity) and experimenting reveal one fact at a time, *Identify* or a Short Rest of study reveals everything but curses, and attuning reveals a curse (table rule). A card tracks every item the party doesn't fully understand yet. |
| **Encumbrance without weights** | Paste an inventory (from D&D Beyond or typed, one item per line, counts like "2 Torches" or "Dagger x3" understood). Each weight is estimated: from the 2024 PHB equipment list first (SRD 5.2), then from the name ("…sword", "ring", "potion"), else 1 lb. Weights in the pasted text are used as is, and any line can be corrected. Rations (2 lb per day), water, coins (50 per pound) and magic items are added automatically. Capacity is Strength × 15 lb for Small or Medium creatures (scaled by size), mounts use their PHB capacities, and going over capacity shows with the debuffs. Carrying capacity and the variant rule (table rule, Gritty tier) can each be switched off: Encumbered over 5 × Strength (speed −10 ft) and Heavily Encumbered over 10 × Strength (speed −20 ft, Disadvantage on Strength, Dexterity and Constitution d20 tests). |
| **Coins, containers and storage** | Each character's purse holds coins by denomination: spending makes change, debt is tracked, coins weigh 50 to the pound, and a moneychanger swaps denominations (optional fee). Pasted prices are kept as item values. Inventory items can be packed into containers (backpacks, sacks, chests, quivers) with capacity shown, and anything inside a Bag of Holding, Handy Haversack or Portable Hole adds no weight. The Storage tab holds stashes, bank vaults and vehicles (cart, wagon, keelboat, sailing ship and more, with cargo capacity), and items (with their contents) and coins move between them and the characters. |
| **Ammunition and crafted goods** | Arrows, bolts, sling bullets and needles in an inventory are counted one by one. Firing records what was spent, and after a fight a minute of searching recovers half, rounded down, for one character or the whole party. Finished crafting projects put the item, potions, scrolls or (identified) magic item straight into the crafter's inventory. |
| **Night watch** | Plan the night as 2, 3, 4 or 8 watches with clock times, assign people (pairs allowed) or share them out evenly within each person's limit. Each watcher's passive Perception is shown for the light during their watch: −5 in Dim Light (twilight or a full moon) or in Darkness seen through Darkvision, and −5 for hearing only in Darkness without it, unless a fire or light source lasts the whole watch. Heavy rain or snow and sandstorms give −5 whatever the light (Disadvantage doesn't stack). Darkvision counts from species, the *Darkvision* spell and Goggles of Night, and Devil's Sight sees through Darkness. A Long Rest allows only 2 hours of watch (4 for characters who Trance), so anyone who watches longer gets no Long Rest benefits. There's an optional encounter check per watch. |
| **Effects and lights** | Torches, candles, lamps and lanterns burn down (with a warning 10 minutes before they go out, and +1 flask of oil). Spells, potions and long effects (*Mage Armor*, *Aid*, *Heroes' Feast*, *Goodberry*, *Geas* and more) expire on the clock. Each caster holds one Concentration spell at a time. *Leomund's Tiny Hut*, *Rope Trick* and *Magnificent Mansion* count as shelter from the weather. The header shows the soonest-ending effect. |
| **Exploration** | 10-minute exploration turns with an optional wandering monster check, hold-breath time (1 + Con modifier minutes, at least 30 seconds), and frigid water (safe for Con score minutes, then a DC 10 Con save each minute). |
| **Money and mounts** | A gold ledger per character. In town, daily lifestyle is charged at midnight and covers meals and lodging (Wretched free up to Aristocratic 10 GP). Hireling wages (skilled 2 GP, untrained 2 SP a day), crafting materials and Bastion construction are charged too, and all of it can be switched off. Mounts (horses, ponies, mules, camels, mastiffs and more) are party members with their own feed, water and Exhaustion. Buying and selling magic items are downtime deals, with typical values by rarity and selling at about half. |
| **World** | Moon phases (Selûne, with an offset to match your campaign), festivals and custom holidays, faction renown with notes (2024 DMG: missions worth +1 to +4, group involvement in downtime at 10 × renown days for +1, and the Level-Based Renown table), with marks of prestige granted by a faction tied to its renown (table rule: loyal service +1, a mark taken back −2, offenses against the faction cost the marks trust, and optional renown minimums for titles and fortifications), spells cast daily until permanent (*Teleportation Circle*, *Guards and Wards*, *Private Sanctum* for 365 days; *Forbiddance* for 30), resurrection time limits (*Revivify* 1 minute, *Raise Dead* and *Reincarnate* 10 days, *Resurrection* 100 years) with *Gentle Repose* pausing the count, and divination limits (+25% chance of a wrong answer per extra casting before a Long Rest). |
| **Downtime extras** | Activity presets (carousing, crime, gambling, pit fighting, relaxation, religious service, research, training, working) with suggested time, cost and the check to make, plus replacing a Wizard's lost spellbook (1 hour and 10 GP per prepared spell level). |
| **Running a business** (optional 2014 DMG rule, switchable) | Track shops, inns, farms, temples and other holdings with their daily maintenance cost. Each downtime day the owner spends running one counts (at most 30); then roll d100 + days on the Running a Business table to pay 1½×, full or half the maintenance, break even, or earn 1d6, 2d8 or 3d10 × 5 GP. A cost the owner can't pay becomes a debt that gives −10 to later rolls until it's paid, and each business keeps a running net profit. Businesses can also be built from scratch: the 2014 DMG Building a Stronghold costs and times (guildhall or trading post 5,000 GP and 60 days, noble estate 25,000 GP and 150 days, up to a palace) plus table-rule estimates for a market stall, shop, workshop, tavern, farm, inn and shrine, all editable, with an optional land price. Construction is paid up front, advances each day while the owner oversees it, and goes a third as fast while they're away; it opens for business when done. Construction can be sped up (table rules): up to 2 extra crews (each +50% pace for half the construction price of the work left), party members working on site (+5% each, +10% with carpenter's or mason's tools), and construction spells cast on site using a slot (*Stone Shape* 1 day, *Fabricate* 2, *Move Earth* 3, *Wall of Stone* 5). The same options work on Bastion facility building, enlarging and defensive walls. Crafting projects (nonmagical items and Potions of Healing) can take hired artisans at 2 GP a workday, who count as assistants dividing the time (2024 PHB: normally one assistant, more if the DM allows), and *Fabricate* finishes a nonmagical item from its raw materials in 10 minutes. |
| **Hunting** | Pick a kill from built-in game animals or any creature in a 5etools bestiary you import. Butchering gives raw meat by size (table rule: Tiny 1 lb up to Gargantuan 4,000 lb) into the camp stores, with an optional DC 10 Survival check to avoid wasting half. Big beasts leave hides for crafting, and constructs, elementals, oozes and undead give nothing. |
| **Crafting materials** | A stash of materials with amounts and values. Projects can use them, and their value counts toward the cost, with the rest paid in gold. |
| **Marks of Prestige** (2024 DMG, World tab) | Fortifications cost their DMG upkeep each day (outpost or watchtower 50 GP, keep 100 GP, castle 400 GP) once the grantor stops paying, and can become the holder's Bastion. Special training on the Downtime tab: 30 days with the trainer for a skill, a tool or a language, recorded on the character. Table rules (switchable): the grantor's trust (3 down to 0, when the mark is taken back); parcels of land pay monthly rents (farmland 2d6 × 5 GP, a village 2d6 × 15 GP) but a 1 on a d6 brings trouble that costs trust if left for 30 days; titles must be lived up to (Comfortable for a knight up to Aristocratic for a duke) and can come with land and be passed to an heir; special rights give free room and board in a named town (up to Comfortable), sworn soldiers as unpaid guards, or a letter of marque (the crown takes 10% of a prize), and run out when the document says. |
| **Renown, fear and stress** | Per-character trackers with reasons logged. Stress and Fear show with the debuffs, and a Long Rest eases each by an amount you choose. |
| **Getting lost** | Off-road travel calls for a navigator's Wisdom (Survival) check against the terrain: DC 5 for grassland, 10 for arctic, desert, hills or sea under clear skies, and 15 for forest, jungle, swamp, mountains or sea under clouds. Roads, rivers and coasts need no check. Slow pace gives Advantage and fast pace Disadvantage, and (table rule) heavy rain or snow and darkness each add +5. On a failure the party wanders off course unknowingly, and the navigator can try again every 1d6 travel hours. |
| **Telling time by nature** | Each part of the day has its sky cues (shadows, birdsong, dawn colors, stars). They get vaguer under cloud and vanish underground. "No timepiece" mode shows these cues instead of the clock (the DM can peek), and a "Read the sky" Wisdom (Survival) check (DC 10 clear, 15 overcast, 20 underground) gives a guess that's off by 1d4 hours on a failure. |
| **What characters feel** | Every status change over time (thirst, hunger, cold, food poisoning, dying, waking, stress, fear, lights going out and more) comes with an in-world description the DM can read out. A separate status history keeps every change per character, and the Chronicle has a player view with only the descriptions. |
| **Interrupted rests and waking** | Interrupt a rest partway: combat, casting a non-cantrip spell, taking damage or an hour of exertion breaks it. A Long Rest interrupted after at least 1 hour gives Short Rest benefits, or the party can resume for 1 extra hour. A noise only wakes people, and the rest continues. Whoever is on watch is awake, a loud noise wakes everyone, and quiet threats are noticed with passive Perception − 5 while asleep (table rule); anyone else has to be shaken awake. Short Rests now need at least 1 HP. |
| **Hirelings with jobs** | Torchbearers, porters, laborers, guards, guides, cooks, grooms, healers, artisans, scribes, interpreters, teamsters, sailors and messengers. | **Journeys and hazards** | Plan a trip in stages and run each one day by day, camping each night. A fully mounted party can gallop for an hour at double distance. | **Gifts, walls and libraries** | Supernatural Blessings, Boons, Charms (with uses) and Curses. Bastion defensive walls cost 250 GP and take 10 days per square. | **Diseases** | The 2024 DMG magical contagions. A cursed item's attunement can't be ended until Remove Curse. |
| **Shop and loot** | All 2024 PHB equipment prices for buying into the inventory, selling at half (full value for gems, art and trade goods) and splitting coin to the copper. Table rules: what villages, towns and cities stock, a 10% discount through a Renown 3+ faction, and potions and scrolls through a Renown 10+ faction. |
| **Downtime outcomes** (optional Xanathar's rules, switchable) | Carousing, crime, gambling, pit fighting, religious service, research and working a trade roll their checks when finished and apply gold, debts, jail time, contacts, favors and lore (into the character's notes), with a 10% chance of a complication. Relaxation grants Advantage against lingering disease and poison. Training (10 workweeks minus the Intelligence modifier, 25 GP a week) adds the language or tool to the character. |
| **Obstacles, snow and mud** | Journey stages can carry DMG obstacles (cliffs, blizzards, forest fires, swamps, rapids, floods, rockslides, sandstorms) passed by a leader's check or a group check; failure costs hours, Exhaustion, damage or a condition. Table rule: snow builds up and melts with the weather, mud follows rain, and a foot of snow or deep mud off a good road makes travel Difficult Terrain, with a notice when it starts and ends. |
| **Encounter frequency** | Location danger controls check cadence and the d20 threshold. A Moderate site checks every four hours of travel, two hours in an exposed camp or rest, and four hours in a sheltered camp or rest. A stuck party checks at the exposed rate and adds +2. Obvious signs such as noise or a trail add +1 while stopped. An ordinary cave offers weather protection and uses the exposed encounter rate unless marked concealed or defensible. Downed companions cannot stand watch and may slow travel when they need to be carried. The Encounters tab shows the odds of any result, a creature group and a Hostile group under current conditions. |
| **NPC Loyalty** | Under Town & Money, track the 2024 DMG Loyalty score for intelligent NPCs who join as volunteers, hirelings, or companions. They start at half the party’s highest player-character Charisma score. Aid can raise Loyalty and mistreatment can lower it. At 0 they leave. As a table rule, paid NPCs return half their latest recorded payment to the original payers, rounded down per payer and limited to available coin if the NPC has a tracked purse. |
| **Encounters, lifestyle and renown** | Hostile encounters roll the creatures' Stealth (estimated from CR) against the party's best passive Perception, adjusted for pace and for what each person can see (light, Darkvision, moonlight, heavy rain or snow); a surprised party has Disadvantage on Initiative, and non-hostile creatures can be parleyed with. Table rule: once a week in town, Wretched and Squalid living risks theft, beatings and Sewer Plague, and wealth draws attention; Renown 3+ means shelter in need. Faction missions add renown, and level-based renown follows a character. Mixing potions from the inventory uses both up and applies the result. |
| **Setting-book Bastion facilities** | Eighteen facilities from *Forgotten Realms: Heroes of Faerûn* and *Eberron: Forge of the Artificer* (2025), each switchable by book: Red Wizard undead defenders that return 14 days after being destroyed, Emerald Enclave grove creatures and Construct Forge constructs as defenders, Harper and Amethyst Dragon training, Zhentarim reconnaissance (Advantage to forage and navigate) and a faster maximum pace after resting there, tournaments that raise Order of the Gauntlet renown (and a knight who cuts attack losses), visiting nobles, Heroic Inspiration, Kundarak trade, Artificer's Forge crafting, manifest and museum Charms, and mobile Bastion helms. |
| **Rivals and complications** | Xanathar's rivals with goals, assets and plans. Each time downtime resolves, a rival advances a plan, with table-rule effects: rumors cost renown, sabotage hurts a business or facility, bribed officials make the next crime harder, toughs take coin, complaints become fines. Pressure 3, 6 and 9 call for the rival to act in person. Every downtime activity has its complication table (my short versions) with effects such as lost coin, a noble's debts, jail, or cut and missing wages, naming a rival where fitting. |
| **Debts** (table rule) | Borrow from lawful lenders, criminals, noble houses or temples, with weekly interest and due dates. Overdue debts escalate by creditor: notices, then bailiffs seizing coin and goods at half value, then debtors' prison worked off at a laborer's wage; or threats, enforcers, an unpaid job that cuts the debt, then violence and a new rival; noble disgrace (renown −1) and seizure; temple penance and quests. Small debts stay at warnings, gambling losses become debts to the house, and a negative purse becomes a debt at the day's end. |
| **Tasha's patrons, retraining and regions** | Group patrons (academy, ancient being, aristocrat, criminal syndicate, guild, military, religious order, sovereign) with their perks applied: pay, lodging, shop and sale discounts, cheaper or faster training, free spellcasting, assignment shares, Common items and Group Assistance. Changing subclass as downtime (twice the new level in days, 100 GP per level). Supernatural regions roll their effect table on triggers (and optionally each dawn), with short summaries and lasting results applied: no rest, failed rests, lost hours, hourly Exhaustion, storms and disease. |
| **Ways around it** | Every mechanic that challenges the party, makes them wait or costs them something (survival, weather, rests and saves, travel, camp and gear, magic, money, downtime and Bastions: 55 in all) has a **🗝 Ways around this** panel in its card. It lists the 2024 spells, class and subclass features, feats, species traits, Eldritch Invocations, gear, magic items and Bastion facilities that shortcut or avoid it, sorted into sections, with what the party can do right now on top. Each option shows its level, spell lists, casting time, costly components and ⚠ fine print (for example, casting *Tiny Hut* during a Long Rest interrupts it, and *Rope Trick* isn't a ritual in 2024). Points tables argue about get a ⚖ ruling: the rules-as-written answer from the 2024 text, plus the common table ruling where many tables differ (*Fire Shield* timed for the hourly cold save, *Greater Restoration* against Exhaustion from hunger, whether a familiar can watch all night, whether *Prayer of Healing* triggers Short Rest features). Cards with several topics have chips, the most pressing first. Party matches use spells, class, level, species, subclass, feats and invocations; a match that depends on a choice the tracker doesn't know says so ("if their subclass is Circle of the Land"). The **Ways Around** tab groups every topic, links back to the card where it lives, and has search and a "party can use now" filter. Checked against the 2024 rules data published by 5etools; Artificer options come from *Eberron: Forge of the Artificer*. |
| **Lingering injuries** (optional 2014 DMG rule, Brutal tier) | Dropping to 0 HP or failing a death save by 5 or more rolls the d20 table (lost eye or limb, limp, internal injury, broken ribs, horrible or minor scar, festering wound), and the DM rolls one for a critical hit. Effects and cures are the DMG's, with nothing added. A festering wound costs 1 HP maximum every day until healed by magic or ten successful DC 15 Medicine checks (one a day); internal injuries and broken ribs heal after ten days of rest (a Long Rest after a day without travel); Cure Wounds, Healing Word and other magical healing end most of them, Heal removes a horrible scar and Regenerate restores lost limbs. |
| **Madness** (optional 2014 DMG rule, also in the SRD 5.1, Brutal tier) | When the DM calls for it: short-term (1d10 minutes), long-term (1d10 × 10 hours) and indefinite madness, with effects rolled on the d100 tables or written in. Lesser Restoration ends short- and long-term madness, Greater Restoration indefinite madness, and Remove Curse or Dispel Evil and Good may, depending on the source; Calm Emotions suppresses it. Table rules on top: Stress or Fear reaching 6 calls for a DC 15 Wisdom save against long-term madness, and Heal ends any madness. |
| **Ages and birthdays** | Each character's age, birthday on the campaign calendar and typical lifespan (2024 PHB species, overridable), with a line on each birthday and when they cross into middle age, old age and beyond. Magical aging (a Ghost's Visage, a Potion of Longevity with its growing chance to backfire) is kept separate, and an Archdruid ages a year per ten. |
| **Wear and repair** (table rule, Brutal tier) | Every 8 hours of hard going (arctic, desert, mountain, swamp, Underdark), heavy rain or salt spray adds wear to exposed gear of the materials that suffer from it; at 3 wear the item is damaged. An hour of upkeep clears the wear. Damaged gear is repaired with the matching artisan's tools (DC 10, 1 hour), makeshift (DC 15, 2 hours), Mending, or by an artisan in town for a tenth of its price. Storms and long voyages wear ships' hulls until they make half speed, and a dock repairs them for 1% of the price per point of wear. |
| **Taxes, tithes and dues** (table rule) | Land and property taxes, temple tithes (a share of gold earned since the last one), guild dues, rent and trade licenses, paid by one character or split by the party, charged at midnight when due. What can't be paid becomes a debt to the collector and escalates like any other; three missed guild payments mean expulsion. |
| **Wanted by the law** (table rule) | Crimes raise heat and a bounty in the place they happened (minor, serious, grave). Suspected, Wanted or Hunted, the party may be recognised each day they spend there (a disguise means the roll is made twice, keeping the result better for the party). Heat cools a point a week, faster while they're elsewhere; bribes, paying the bounty as a fine or surrendering to jail clear it. Getting caught at a crime in downtime adds heat on its own. |
| **Contacts and relationships** | People the party knows, with a bond from −3 to +3 that sets their attitude (2024 PHB: Friendly, Indifferent, Hostile). Favors use the 2024 DMG Influence DCs (10 Friendly, 15 Indifferent, +5 for a big ask) or are automatic when owed; doing them a good turn earns a favor. Table rule: Friendly contacts cool after 60 days without word, and won't help twice within 30 days. Allied and hostile contacts from downtime outcomes are added automatically. |

## Deployment

Every push to `main` runs the tests, builds the app and publishes it to GitHub Pages (`.github/workflows/deploy-pages.yml`). You can also start it by hand from the Actions tab. One-time setup: in the repository's **Settings → Pages**, set **Source** to **GitHub Actions**.

## Spell data from 5etools

The app ships with **no** spell text. In **Settings → Spell library**, load a spell JSON file in 5etools format that you have access to (for example `spells-xphb.json`). Only the name, level, school, source and ritual/concentration flags are kept, and you can then search the list on the Morning tab. You can also add spells by hand.

## Roadmap

Roll up a settlement on the Town & Money tab to use named service providers, inn rates and mount boarding. Pick up fixed storage and held replies where you left them.

Use **Settings → Start an Eberron campaign** to begin in Sharn or Wroat. View skycoach and lightning rail departures on the Dashboard or Travel tab.

The Encounters tab can export the original rolls, party roster and conditions of a creature encounter as JSON. Import into an open Owlbear scene is still planned.

See [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) for the complete roadmap and implementation status.

## Development

```bash
npm install
npm run dev        # http://localhost:5173
npm test           # rules and engine tests (Vitest)
npm run build      # static site in dist/
```

The rules live in `src/rules/` as pure functions. `src/state/engine.ts` applies them to the game state (clock ticks, saves, rests, travel, meals). The React UI is in `src/components/`.

## Table defaults you may want to change

- **Seasonal base temperatures** per climate (`src/rules/weather.ts`). The DMG only says "normal for the season", so these are reasonable real-world-style defaults.
- **Climate-adjusted precipitation**, the **campfire warms the camp** rule, and **double water in extreme heat** (2014 rule) are toggles in Settings.
- **Raw food** (Con save or Sick until a Long Rest), **unpurified water** (DC 10 Con save or Sick until a Long Rest), **boiling water** (30 min) and **cooking makes foraged food safe** are table rules. The 2024 books leave food safety to the DM.
- **Firewood gathering** (1d6 + 2 hours of fuel on a Survival check) is a table rule. The 2024 books don't specify one.
